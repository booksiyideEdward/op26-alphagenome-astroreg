# AI-use policy audit and disclosure

Policy check date: 13 August 2026.

## bioRxiv

The official bioRxiv About and screening-procedure pages describe scope, submission screening, plagiarism checks and author metadata requirements. A separate official rule prescribing manuscript placement or submission-form disclosure for generative-AI assistance was not located in those current public materials. This is an absence-of-located-rule statement, not a claim that bioRxiv prohibits or endorses a particular use.

- https://www.biorxiv.org/about-biorxiv
- https://connect.biorxiv.org/news/2022/06/13/screening_procedures

OP26 should nevertheless carry a manuscript disclosure because the use exceeded copy editing and the plausible target journals require disclosure.

## Target-journal policies

The final target journal is not fixed.

- **Nucleic Acids Research:** AI used to generate content, write code or analyze data must be disclosed in both the cover letter and the Methods or Acknowledgements. https://academic.oup.com/nar/pages/policies
- **PLOS Computational Biology:** AI contributions must be reported in a dedicated Methods section, or Acknowledgements when an article type has no Methods. The disclosure should identify tools, describe use and validation, and identify affected study/article/data/supporting-file components. https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/s/ethical-publishing-practice
- **Nature Portfolio:** AI systems cannot be authors; substantive LLM use should be documented according to the portfolio AI policy. https://www.nature.com/nature-portfolio/editorial-policies/ai

## Minimum truthful manuscript disclosure

> Generative-AI tools, including OpenAI Codex and DeepSeek, assisted selected aspects of code development, literature triage, analysis planning and auditing, and manuscript drafting and editing. AI-assisted code and numerical outputs were checked through executable reproductions, cohort-cardinality and identifier-reconciliation checks, and results-to-manuscript comparisons; bibliographic and policy statements were checked against primary or publisher sources. No AI system is an author. Human author review and approval of the final submitted version remain required.

Recommended placement: a standalone `Use of generative AI` section after Limitations and before Data and code availability. For NAR, repeat or cross-reference the disclosure in the cover letter. Any submission-form AI question must be answered consistently.

The wording names the documented tools and substantive roles without describing the study as autonomous. It does not claim that human review is already complete.
